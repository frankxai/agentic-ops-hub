# 02 — Vertical wedges

## Selection method

Pick a vertical **only** when the same structured workflow can serve at least five plausible customers with limited configuration.

Score each proposed wedge 1–5:

| Factor | Question |
|---|---|
| Pain / frequency | Does a real operator repeat it weekly? |
| Budget authority | Can the buyer pay from an existing budget? |
| Data/connector access | Can v1 work with exports/manual upload before deep integrations? |
| Outcome measurability | Can we show saved time, conversion, risk reduction, or revenue? |
| Repeatability | Does 80% of workflow stay the same across clients? |
| Distribution edge | Does the estate have audience, expertise, partner or proof? |
| Risk fit | Can actions be bounded and human-approved? |

**Proceed:** ≥24/35, no risk score <3.  
**Kill/Pivot:** less than 5 qualified conversations, no pilot deposit, or no repeatable workflow after two paid implementations.

## Recommended initial wedge order

This is a strategy recommendation based on estate assets—not a claim of validated demand. Validate with 10 interviews before build.

| Priority | Wedge | Named buyer + recurring job | First paid deliverable | Why the estate can win | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Creator Product Engine** | creator/educator turns expertise into a sellable product and distribution loop | guided install + Creator Launch Kit | GenCreator, ACOS, CIS, FrankX content and product proof | generic content-generator subscription |
| 2 | **AI-Native Expert Practice** | small advisory/agency turns client discovery + delivery into a repeatable workflow | Practice Intake & Delivery Kit | App Factory, agent ops, design/eval discipline | selling uncontrolled client-facing agent access |
| 3 | **Energy / field-sales intelligence** | installer/advisor qualifies leads and produces a trusted recommendation | scoped vertical pilot | PV Lager domain evidence and Energy IS direction | regulated claims without domain owner and review |
| 4 | **Creator wealth / product portfolio** | creator chooses/ships owned revenue assets | DPI diagnostic + implementation cohort | DPI decision matrices and honest income doctrine | "make money with AI" hype funnels |
| 5 | **Brand content operations for teams** | brand team routes approved assets from brief through review and distribution | governed content-ops installation | ACOS/CIS/SDI and trust/audit approach | another generic social scheduler |

## Product wedge canvas

Copy `templates/VERTICAL-WEDGE.md` into the proposed product repo before building.

A good wedge statement:

> For **[specific operator]** who needs to **[repeated expensive job]**, **[product]** produces **[verifiable output]** in **[time]**, with **[human approval/trust boundary]**—unlike **[current workaround]**.

## Vertical implementation sequence

1. Interview ten buyers; get their artifacts/screenshots, not just opinions.
2. Map the current workflow: trigger → inputs → work → approval → output → measurement.
3. Sell a paid discovery or fixed pilot before custom code.
4. Deliver using the shared core plus a small vertical module.
5. Capture before/after evidence and customer language with consent.
6. Extract the common 80%; turn it into an Outcome Kit.
7. Only then open recurring SaaS/AaaS subscriptions.

## Compliance boundary

For finance, legal, health, energy, hiring, publishing, payments, or regulated recommendations:

- domain professional owns final decision;
- sources and assumptions remain inspectable;
- tool actions are allowlisted;
- sensitive data boundary is explicit;
- no regulated outcome claim is generated as unreviewed truth.
