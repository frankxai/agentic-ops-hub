# 07 — Measurements and decision log

## North-star hierarchy

| Level | Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | verified outcome completion | customer received actual value |
| Product | time to first success | proves productization |
| Economics | gross margin after support/model cost | proves business viability |
| Retention | repeat outcome / renewal / expansion | proves ongoing job |
| Distribution | qualified conversations from owned proof | proves a non-paid growth path |
| System | reusable module percentage | proves empire, not agency work |

## Stage gates

### Discovery → pilot

- 10 meaningful interviews or equivalent strongly sourced workflow evidence
- repeated language for the same painful job
- buyer can name a budget/process owner
- paid pilot or decisive paid-discovery signal

### Pilot → Outcome Kit

- at least two deployments or one deployment plus independently reproducible result
- ≥70% workflow shared
- evidence, onboarding and support boundary documented

### Outcome Kit → recurring AaaS

- recurring job/usage exists
- a customer has paid to keep access/outcomes active
- gross margin supports operations and model costs
- action boundaries, audit and failure path verified

### AaaS → partner license

- ≥2–3 verified customer outcomes
- module + deployment process repeatable
- customer success and security/compliance posture mature enough for commitment

## Monthly decision log

Copy `templates/EMPIRE-DECISION.md` into the relevant product repo.  
The decision must say **what evidence would change it**. No vanity metrics, no feelings-only portfolio decisions.
