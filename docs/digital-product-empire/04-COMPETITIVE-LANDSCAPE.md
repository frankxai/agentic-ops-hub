|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prompt-to-app builders | Lovable, Bolt, v0, Replit, Base44, FlutterFlow | instant prototypes, broad awareness, speed | generic outputs; weak vertical implementation and governance | **Do not compete head-on.** Use internally; sell outcome kits + vertical systems |

| Creator suites | Adobe GenStudio, Canva, Notion, HubSpot, Jasper and specialists | polished UI, existing distribution, asset production | weaker creator-owned memory, multi-agent governance, portable workflows | GenCreator/CIS: creator-owned workflow + open core + guided outcome |

| AI agencies / dev shops | thousands of local/global firms | custom delivery and relationships | labor-heavy, little reusable IP, uneven safety | productized installs; turn every pilot into a reusable module |
| Vertical SaaS incumbents | domain-specific systems | existing workflow/data and buyer trust | AI often bolted on, changing slowly, poor cross-tool orchestration | wedge into a narrow assistive workflow; integrate rather than replace initially |

## Positioning statement

> **Starlight builds governed, branded AI operating products for a repeated vertical job—then hands the customer a system they can understand, approve, measure, and leave with.**

The differentiation is not “we have agents.” Everyone has agents.

### Moat stack

1. **Vertical evidence:** paid pilots, domain artifacts, measurable before/after.
2. **Outcome packaging:** a 30–60 minute first success, then a bounded install—not a blank builder.
3. **Trust architecture:** allowlists, approval-bound actions, receipts, tenant boundaries, evals.
4. **Design intelligence:** branded, human-tested UI rather than generation aesthetics.
5. **Sovereign exit:** exportable customer artifacts/configuration; avoid hostage architecture.
6. **Compounding delivery:** every pilot improves one shared module, skill, test, and case-study asset.

## Competitive responses

| Buyer objection | Answer with product evidence, not rhetoric |
|---|---|
| “We can build this in Lovable/Replit.” | Yes—if they own product design, architecture, QA, governance and maintenance. Sell the repeatable domain outcome and proof, not coding access. |
| “Microsoft/Salesforce already has agents.” | Keep their system of record; deliver the missing narrow job and integrate via approved adapters. |
| “Why not hire an agency?” | Fixed-scope install, shared maintained core, visible handoff, timeboxed outcome, and no bespoke-dependency bait. |
| “Why not automate it in Zapier/n8n?” | Use automation for plumbing. The value is the domain model, approval flow, UX, evidence and operations—not another workflow graph. |
| “Why pay for templates?” | Do not sell templates. Sell a versioned operational asset with onboarding, verification, update path and a bounded outcome. |

## Competitive intelligence SOP

Before positioning any paid offer, create 5–10 dated competitor cards:

1. Direct buyer alternative
2. General builder alternative
3. Incumbent/system-of-record alternative
4. DIY/manual workaround
5. At least one niche/vertical specialist

Use primary sources, record observed date, and keep legal/trademark claims out of marketing copy unless professionally reviewed.
