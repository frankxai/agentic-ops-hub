## Taste Correction

Frank does not want negative clickbait openings like "Stop ...". The operating language for this carousel is:

- Positive first-read
- Grateful and meaningful tone
- Dopamine hook through aspiration, identity, and specificity
- Founder-to-founder intelligence
- Deep AI craft, business leverage, trust, and proof
- Human emotion without soft generic inspiration

The creative translation of the requested "Vogue + Meta + Higgsfield" direction is: global fashion editorial polish, premium social product-system clarity, and cinematic AI-film craft. No direct brand imitation, no logos, no fake magazine marks.

## Copy Direction

Primary post opener:

> I am grateful we get to build in a moment like this.
>
> AI agents are no longer just clever tools we prompt once and forget. Used well, they become a living operating layer around our ideas: helping us research, build, verify, document, and move with more care.
>
> The real craft is not asking for more output. It is designing the system where intelligence becomes trust: clear roles, meaningful ownership, visible proof, and human approval for the moments that matter.

Headline bank for future variants:

- The future we get to build
- A more human way to work with AI agents
- From tools to teammates
- Proof is how intelligence becomes trust
- Build a company that learns every day
- The new craft is designing the operating system
- Better agents begin with better responsibility

Avoid:

- Stop ...
- Nobody is talking about ...
- You are doing AI wrong
- This changes everything
- Generic unlock/transform language
- Empty hustle or fear-driven urgency
