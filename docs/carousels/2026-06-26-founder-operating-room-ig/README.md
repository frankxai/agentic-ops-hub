## Creative Thesis

Founder Operating Room is a premium educational carousel about turning AI agents, Slack, repos, websites, content, and approvals into a calmer operating system for a founder-led company.

This is not an AI tips post. It is a tiny agency campaign: editorial founder world, specific operating artifacts, precise positive copy, and motion-ready structure.

## Taste Rules Applied

- Positive and grateful framing, no fear hook.
- Specific AI operations instead of generic AI inspiration.
- Tactile editorial artifacts instead of abstract glow.
- Human approval and proof treated as premium trust signals.
- Generated visuals are text-free foundations; exact typography is rendered deterministically.
- Motion-ready composition: each slide works as a still, but the sequence can become a Reel/storyboard.

## Platform Notes

- Organic Instagram carousel is built as 4:5 portrait, 1080 x 1350.
- All slides use the same aspect ratio to avoid unwanted mixed-crop behavior.
- Important copy stays away from hard edges and remains visible in thumbnail/contact-sheet review.
