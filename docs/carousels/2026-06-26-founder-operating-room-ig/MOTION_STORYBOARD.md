# Motion Storyboard

Motion Director thesis: motion should make the operating system feel calm, intentional, and inspectable. The signature move is not a generic fade. It is a proof trail: the viewer sees an insight become a card, the card enters a Slack room, the work collects proof, and the final post earns approval.

## Surface

- Primary still surface: Instagram 4:5 carousel.
- Motion derivative: 9:16 Reel / Story.
- Secondary cuts: LinkedIn carousel, YouTube short teaser, 16:9 deck opener.

## Motion Personality

- Precise
- Calm
- Editorial
- Trustworthy
- Founder-led

## Shot List

1. Poster frame: founder operating room.
   - Motion: slow camera push, paper artifacts reveal with shallow parallax.
   - Text hold: "A calmer company. A smarter system."

2. System map.
   - Motion: signal card enters, then stages light in sequence: Signal, Brief, Build, Proof, Approve, Publish.
   - Job: teach process, not decorate.

3. Slack cockpit.
   - Motion: channel cards slide into assigned lanes, one approval card locks into place.
   - Job: show Slack as decision architecture.

4. Agent ownership.
   - Motion: four rule cards appear with measured cadence: Role, Room, Proof, Gate.
   - Job: show governance before autonomy.

5. Proof wall.
   - Motion: proof checks illuminate one by one.
   - Job: make trust feel earned.

6. Content studio.
   - Motion: research note becomes brief, brief becomes style frame, style frame becomes carousel.
   - Job: connect content craft to operating process.

7. Format system.
   - Motion: one master frame re-crops into 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, and 1:1.
   - Job: show one idea becoming many platform cuts.

8. Close.
   - Motion: all paths settle into the Founder OS; CTA card holds.
   - Job: calm close, no hype.

## Runtime Direction

- Preferred implementation: Remotion or GSAP timeline.
- Duration: 18-24 seconds.
- Frame rate: 30fps.
- Easing: slow out / precise snap, no bouncy social template energy.
- Reduced-motion fallback: static carousel slides.
- Export: MP4 H.264 1080 x 1920 for Reel; poster frame from slide 1.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Still slides remain strong without motion.
- Motion clarifies sequence and causality.
- Text is readable during movement.
- Loop resolves cleanly if used as a teaser.
- No motion-only information.
- No public posting without explicit approval.
