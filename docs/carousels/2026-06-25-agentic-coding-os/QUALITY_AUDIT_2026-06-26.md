# Quality Audit: Agentic Coding OS Carousel

Date: 2026-06-26

Verdict: `REBUILD_REQUIRED`

This pack is no longer considered approval-ready. It remains useful as a
production proof and source-backed draft, but it does not meet the social-team
quality bar.

## What Works

- The topic is useful: agents need operating spaces, proof, and approval gates.
- The claim-risk boundary is responsible.
- Exact text is deterministic.
- PDF, PNG sequence, JPG sequence, contact sheet, and generated cover asset now
  exist.
- The Slack-first mistake was corrected conceptually.

## What Fails

- The deck looks like an internal technical export, not a polished social
  campaign.
- The visual language is too templated: repeated dark panels, small labels, and
  weak pacing.
- The first slide is clear but not memorable enough to carry a public campaign.
- The generated cover concept is generic; it does not feel proprietary to
  FrankX or AI-Architect.
- The export has a footer/crop defect.
- The scoring framework allowed "pass with notes" even though the notes affect
  public confidence.
- The approval packet overstated readiness.

## Decision

- Do not route this as approval-ready.
- Do not generate more one-off cover images against this deck.
- Rebuild the carousel from a new `CREATIVE_DIRECTION.md`.
- Keep the current pack as a learning artifact and production proof.

## Rebuild Direction

The new version should feel like a social-native operating-system playbook, not
a docs export.

Required changes:

- Make the first frame iconic: a sharp claim plus one proprietary visual motif.
- Replace repeated dark card layouts with varied editorial compositions.
- Use one strong visual system: operating rooms, proof packets, lanes, and
  approval gates, but stylized enough to feel owned.
- Use generated imagery only if it is campaign-defining; otherwise use
  deterministic high-design graphics.
- Build LinkedIn and Instagram as related siblings, not one deck resized.
- Require 90/100 social-confidence score before approval routing.
