# Brand Social Guide: FrankX / AI-Architect / Builder Education

Status: reusable lane foundation.

## Brand Relationship

FrankX is the public authority and founder-facing demand engine.
AI-Architect is the enterprise/professional lane for AI CoE, Oracle, governance,
and implementation credibility. Builder Education is the practical teaching lane
for Codex, Claude Code, ChatGPT, GitHub, agentic coding, and operating systems.

Treat AI-Architect and Builder Education as lane-specific expressions under the
FrankX practical authority umbrella unless a dedicated brand pack exists.

## Audience

| Lane | Audience | They Want |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FrankX | founders, operators, creator-founders | leverage, systems, revenue, clarity |
| AI-Architect | AI CoE leaders, enterprise architects, Oracle ecosystem | governance, adoption, risk control, implementation patterns |
| Builder Education | technical founders, builders, agentic dev operators | repeatable workflows, templates, examples, tool judgment |

## Promise

Help serious builders turn AI capability into owned systems, working products,
revenue loops, and governed leverage.

## Voice

- direct, commercial, founder-to-founder
- mechanism before motivation
- practical before poetic
- strategic without corporate fog
- confident, but proof-aware
- specific about tools, repos, gates, and workflows

## Visual Identity

Use the FrankX dual-spectrum posture:

- obsidian/void foundation
- emerald/cyan for technical rigor, systems, automation, and tooling
- amber/gold for approval, judgment, craft, and human gatekeeping
- restrained grids, borders, rails, and evidence motifs

Use AI-Architect with slightly more enterprise restraint:

- more whitespace
- less punchline energy
- stronger governance language
- diagrams and decision tables over hype

Use Builder Education with more tactical clarity:

- step-by-step patterns
- "copy this" structures
- visible work orders, checklists, and packets
- fewer abstractions per slide

## Positioning Pillars

1. Agentic work needs an operating system.
2. Humans approve irreversible moves.
3. Proof beats vibes.
4. Tools need lanes.
5. Content becomes an asset only when it is reusable.
6. Governance is leverage, not bureaucracy.

## Do

- name the mechanism
- show the workflow
- include the approval boundary
- cite official sources for current claims
- make one concrete useful artifact per post
- turn good posts into templates

## Avoid

- "AI will change everything" without a mechanism
- pretending Slack is the only possible operating cockpit
- claiming autonomous execution without active proof
- fake screenshots, fake dashboards, or fake logos
- generated images with final exact text
- tiny labels and overpacked architecture diagrams

## Reusable CTA Bank

- Comment `agent OS` if you want the founder setup template.
- Save this as the operating pattern for your AI team.
- Use this as the first approval gate in your agent workflow.
- Steal the packet, not the vibe.
- Build the system before you let agents act.

## Review Gate

Before approval:

- Does the audience know the next action?
- Is the mechanism named?
- Is the irreversible-action gate explicit?
- Are current factual claims source-backed?
- Can this become a reusable client/community template?
