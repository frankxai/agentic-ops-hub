## Current Source Posture

  photorealistic content, including realistic scenes that did not occur:
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- LinkedIn supports document posts including PDF, with a 100 MB and 300 page
  ceiling. PDF remains the preferred deterministic carousel export:
  https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a518909
- Instagram feed carousel support remains official: up to 20 photos and videos
  can be selected for a multi-photo/video post:
  https://help.instagram.com/269314186824048/

  Use it as an iterative concept/edit/QA workflow, not as final exact text:
  https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation
- Income or affiliate-style content needs material-connection disclosure when a
  financial, employment, gift, or similar relationship exists:
