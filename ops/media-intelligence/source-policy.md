# SMIS Source, Rights, and Automation Policy

## Claim classes

Every load-bearing statement is one of:

1. **External source** — direct primary URL, publisher/author, retrieval time, and claim scope.
2. **First-party observation** — method, artifact, date, owner, and limitation.
3. **Original analysis** — inputs named and clearly attributed to the author.
4. **Unsupported** — cannot enter an approval-ready package.

## Reuse rules

- Do not repost another creator's material, screenshots, b-roll, audio, or work without a recorded permission/license/fair-use rationale and platform-specific review.
- Links and citations are not a commercial reuse license.
- Model/tool outputs require a time-bounded policy-evidence record before a commercial production claim or automated use.
- `unknown` commercial use, output ownership, API rights, resale rights, or data retention is a block for unattended production.

## Automation rules

- Direct APIs and schedulers require scoped account authorization, a human-approved package, unique idempotency key, quota/budget check, and an auditable receipt.
- TikTok consent/disclosure requirements and X duplicate/spam controls are adapter-level hard gates.
- No browser automation may be used to bypass an unavailable API, app review, consent flow, or platform terms.
- Stage 0 is draft-only. The daily Media Pulse has no permission to publish or schedule content.

## Promotion evidence

An adapter/tool may move from research to a real-brand pilot only after: source/terms review, test account/synthetic content validation, credential-storage review, receipt/reconciliation proof, export/delete/revoke verification, and an explicit owner decision.
