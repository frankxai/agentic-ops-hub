valuable: recognised, scientifically grounded, swarm quality on par with the
field, monetisable, and visibly enforcing quality across Generals, Specialists,
Workers and kernel, wired into CLI and harness. The 17 October calls stand:
first slice = kernel + hooks pack + CLI; "own repo / updated orgs"; absorb then
retire the vendored hive-mind Queens. A live alignment pass on 19 October found
eight Queen surfaces, two with Registry authority (profile in
starlight-agent-config, runtime and policy in agentic-ops `lifecycle/`), the
placement gate, mirror orgs, review debt and the private-repo Actions budget.

`act` away. claude-flow coordinator names are retired from the repo. Verified at

authority was added; nothing merged; an independent checker is still required.
