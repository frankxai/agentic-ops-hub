# 17 October 2026

## CI economics, free runners, org placement and weekly git-ops

Frank ruled that the GitHub Actions budget stays at $0 and asked for CI to fit free
and already-paid capacity. Measured first, then built.

**Census.** Draft [Ops270](https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-ops/pull/270) adds
`scripts/audit_ci_economics.py`, a read-only REST census of every Registry repo
(draft skip, superseded-run cancel, job timeouts, cron frequency, Vercel ignore step,
7-day runs in per-day windows, billed floor). Last 7 days: at least 18,742 runs. Public
repos used 16,107 of 17,982 wall-clock minutes and bill nothing. Private repos bill at
least 5,231 minutes a week (one job per run, rounded up), against 3,000 included per
month. Four repos hit GitHub's 1,000-result listing cap, so their counts are minimums.
Private CI is currently stopped by the spending limit.

**Templates.** The same PR adds the gencreator.ai pattern as copy-in and
`workflow_call` templates (runner from `vars.CI_RUNNER`, always-run `gate`) and a
Vercel ignored-build script that skips agent-branch previews until the PR is ready.
Codex reviewed adversarially; all seven findings fixed with regression tests (18 pass).

**Runners.** Blacksmith (org-only, 3,000 free minutes per org, per-job Firecracker VMs)
proven on draft
[command-center 79](https://github.com/Starlight-Intelligence/starlight-command-center/pull/79):
all five jobs green on `blacksmith-2vcpu` runners after Frank transferred the repo to
Starlight-Intelligence. Public repos stay on GitHub-hosted runners. The Railway runner
stays held (`railway-ci-runners/docs/RUNNER-RELEASE-BLOCKERS.md`); recommendation is to
retire it. GitHub's self-hosted runner fee was postponed, not in effect.

**Idea repos.** Frank switched Actions off on 37 dormant private repos. Repos with
required checks, secret guards, a Pages site or pushes that week stayed on.

**Weekly git-ops.** Cloud routine "Estate git-ops weekly" (Mondays 06:00 UTC, Sonnet,
no connectors, report-only) comments on a "Weekly estate git-ops report" issue in
agentic-ops. First run 19 October.

**Cost rule.** Native tools before paid connectors is now in the global Claude and
agent instructions, with an `ask` permission on credit-spending connectors.

**Plan page.** The private estate plan (org map, visibility rules, CI/runner matrix,
licensing and contributor model, agent operating model, 90-day order) is an artifact
owned by Frank.

Open, not done: Registry still lists `frankxai/starlight-command-center` and has 39
duplicate rows; the 10 October org proposal ([Ops29](https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-ops/issues/29))
is unratified with six rulings pending; starlightintelligence.ai waits on the Vercel
GitHub App being installed on the org; 68 public repos have no license.
