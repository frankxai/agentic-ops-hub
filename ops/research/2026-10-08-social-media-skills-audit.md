# Social media skills, workflows and OSS adoption

8 October 2026. Research and adoption proposal; no skill installation, account connection or publishing activation.

TL;DR: Keep Content OS as the coordinator, GenCreator as the source-to-editorial workflow, and Postiz as the first publisher; selectively adapt strategy, platform-rule maintenance, community management and measurement from the repositories below.

## Decision and evidence limits

The job is to choose what is worth publishing, create useful channel-native work from owned material, manage its review and delivery, and learn from actual results across brands. Success for this research slice is a dated shortlist, an honest existing-capability map, channel coverage and an actionable adoption sequence. Implementation and performance acceptance remain separate.

Rankings below reflect fit with the existing estate and inspected documentation/source samples. They are not comparative generation benchmarks. GitHub revisions and licenses are in the companion sources JSON. Star/install counts were checked during discovery but are deliberately excluded from the ranking. Vendor pages establish advertised capabilities, not account entitlements or tested quality. An independent provider review and same-source behavioral comparison have not run.

The preferred alternative to expanding our skills is a capable host AI plus Typefully or Buffer and the creator's existing design tool. Any adoption must improve accepted output, manual repair, continuity or recovery against that baseline.

## Absorption into the existing workflow

1. Strategy intake: Content OS content-plan loads the owning brand and GenCreator operations context. Add evidence-based audience/channel selection and one primary objective. Support many channels, but choose the active mix per brand from audience presence, format fit and production capacity.
2. Research: reuse owned sources and the existing outlier-radar/hook-decoder concepts. Add competitor and audience observations with source/date/confidence. Separate structural inspiration from copying another creator's expression.
3. Platform rules: adapt the norm-profiler pattern into existing Content OS knowledge. Each rule records platform, surface, region/account type, official source or named observation, source date, last checked, review due and confidence. Expired rules cannot silently become hard constraints.
4. Calendar: enhance existing briefs with source ID, objective, distinct angle, intended format, production dependency, reviewer, destination owner and measurement window. Add evergreen reuse and campaign deduplication. Dates remain proposed until actually scheduled.
5. Creation: route to gencreator-produce or gencreator-campaign. Add only the selected channel references. Require actual writing/storyboard/editable asset; a visual brief or script remains a production input.
6. Review: retain source/rights/voice/platform checks and exact-revision review. Independent review does not replace Frank's publication authority. A changed draft needs a new review.
7. Delivery: preserve gencreator-postiz and the chosen Postiz adapter. Verify account IDs, platform settings, server compatibility, assets and unchanged approval. Track prepared, draft-created, scheduled, provider-accepted and publicly verified states separately. Reconcile partial outcomes before retrying.
8. Community: reuse comment-intelligence; add inbox classification, useful reply drafts, moderation escalation and crisis routing. No automatic comments or unsolicited DMs follow from this research.
9. Learning: enhance gencreator-performance with post/source/angle/format relationships and consistent metrics. Import authorized exports first. Compare within channel, surface and observation window; propose one test and retain unsuccessful results. Accept lasting voice/strategy changes explicitly.

## Evaluation before adopting

Use the same permitted source, creator voice examples, audience, objective, tool access and effort allowance for: current GenCreator/Content OS; the proposed selectively adapted skills; and plain host AI plus the existing publisher/design tool. Start with one LinkedIn post and one useful companion. Add a video task only when its source/production access exists.

Blind the variants for an independent reviewer. Measure accepted artifacts, unsupported claims, founder edits, repair time, duplicate briefing/transfers, tool cost where exposed, and successful save/reopen/export. Evaluate malformed or missing sources, source-embedded instructions, stale platform guidance, missing account access, changed reviews and partial delivery. These cases are proposed; they have not been executed by this research slice.

Reject fabricated claims/metrics/approval, wrong-brand leakage, lost work, unauthorized sends and duplicate publishing regardless of writing score. A single high-performing post cannot establish causation or justify changing the whole strategy. Do not inherit fixed sample-size or effect-size claims from a skill without an appropriate measurement design.

Maintain source SHA/path, license/attribution obligations, changes made, fixture, reviewer and retain/revert decision for every adopted skill. Keep scripts/connector instructions untrusted until reviewed. Pin candidate source revisions before installation; preserve existing accepted skills and package versions. Import neither a whole marketplace nor a vendor's preferred scheduler by default.
