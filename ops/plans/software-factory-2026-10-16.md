# Software factories for the Starlight estate

Status: implementation program open. This package extends [Ops252](https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-ops/issues/252), the [managed-service proposal, PR244](https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-ops/pull/244), and existing product roadmaps. It does not establish deployed factories, independent review, a paying customer, or superintelligence.

## The outcome

A person should arrive with a question or creative ambition and leave with useful work that survives the session: a researched explanation, a working system, an authored edition, a world, or a learning achievement. They should be able to inspect its sources, change it, compare revisions, control cost and release, and return after an interruption without starting again. The factory exists to make that experience dependable across our websites.

Starlight should make powerful intelligence feel approachable and alive. Its agents should investigate, build, challenge and explain through work the person can inspect. Horizon surfaces invite ambitious exploration; Operator surfaces make the work and its state legible; Academy surfaces develop independent judgment. Agent relationships can be warm and memorable. Their personality never hides a permission, a failed run or an unfinished result.

“Superintelligence” names a research ambition here. The engineering program can demonstrate stronger task performance, memory, tool use and collaboration. It cannot establish that larger claim by naming a fleet or assembling a dashboard.

## Shared platform architecture

1. The existing Next.js/Vercel product handles identity-aware interaction, streaming and artifact review. Keep app-local logic in the product; do not build a generic central backend that secretly becomes every product's authority.
2. A server-owned broker binds authenticated tenant, project, grants, source ACLs, effective budget, tool allowlist and immutable task identity. Model output cannot grant itself permissions. Discovery through MCP is not permission to execute.
3. Use one owning durable workflow. Vercel Workflows suits the accepted app-local path; Cloudflare Agents/Durable Objects remain the accepted option for justified persistent addressable entities. Existing Railway workers remain available for workload-specific process compute. Account entitlement, isolation and actual cost must be proved before adoption.
4. Scoped workers receive an immutable base, exact files, source references, test fixtures and a deadline. Each worktree has one writer. Makers produce candidates; critics identify weaknesses; a separate verifier checks the exact result. A frontier model is used when the task warrants it, with provider/model identity and actual effort recorded rather than inferred from a role name.
5. Store business truth and artifact metadata in the accepted relational boundary. New media delivered by Vercel uses the reviewed Blob/Image default. Drive remains review/recovery evidence, not a production dependency. Apply `architecture_media_fabric_v2`, not the superseded R2-first decision.
6. Emit existing mission envelopes and outcome receipts. Retain attempts, rejected revisions, human edits, unknown external effects and checkpoint references. Retrying a delivery reconciles the previous operation before creating a new one.
7. The CLI, HUD, Observatory and hosted product expose projections of the same work. They do not fabricate progress from process counts. Localhost availability and cloud deployment status are separate observations. A stopped local surface needs a supervised, admitted launch and HTTP application readiness, not a bare link or a port-only success.

## Intelligence that earns greater autonomy

Build capability through a bounded improvement loop: freeze a real customer task and its baseline; let a selected route attempt it; collect the actual artifact and trace; challenge quality and permissions; measure repair effort and full cost; retain what passes; rerun old failure cases before promoting the new route. A rejected customer result remains a failure even when structural tests pass.

Experiments worth pursuing inside the existing products:

- Research-to-artifact workbenches that connect claims, counterevidence, editable designs and executable prototypes. Keep source inspection and scientific confirmation separate.
- Task-scoped expert routing that adds specialists only when independent work can improve the result. Compare against one strong lead using the same tools and sources.
- Permission-aware memory that restores the user's project, decisions and edits across clients, with export/delete and cross-tenant denial. Do not copy private vaults into public demos.
- Multimodal creation with consistent identities, provenance, rights, versioned renditions and human art direction. No generated factual interface text or partner marks disguised as evidence. Higgsfield remains excluded.
- An architecture lab that produces runnable reference implementations, measured alternatives and rollback paths. A diagram or model opinion alone does not validate the design.
- Evaluated skill improvement: a candidate reusable capability passes frozen regression and adversarial cases, receives independent exact-revision review, and enters a versioned release. Agents do not rewrite their own authority or ratify their own performance claims.

These experiments are hypotheses with receiving-user, safety and economic gates. They do not authorize unbounded standing fleets or a new training program.

## Factory operating model

Use a shared talent pool with brand-specific context. A product cell typically needs one delivery lead, a maker, a design/editorial critic, and an independent verifier; architecture, security, accessibility, platform and domain specialists join when the task needs them. Roles can run sequentially. A 13-brand catalog does not imply 13 resident swarms.

The lifecycle is intake → customer/owner framing → research and baseline → experience and architecture decisions → bounded implementation → independent critique and refinement → preview and receiving-user proof → authorized release → observation and recovery → evaluated reuse. A red gate stops the dependent action. It does not close the underlying objective or erase earlier work.

Each work packet names the current issue and product ID, customer result, exact source revision, owner/branch/files, input rights and ACLs, allowed tools and effects, actual model route, cumulative cost and time limits, deliverables, behavioral acceptance, stop conditions and handover. No worker can expand the packet's own grant. See [teams](software-factory-2026-10-16/teams.md) and [rollout](software-factory-2026-10-16/rollout.md).

## Release quality

Every proposed release must establish distinct evidence for availability, complete customer workflow, useful output, brand/design judgment, security/rights, accessibility, recovery, cost and maintainable operation. A provider READY deployment is only a provider state. A screenshot cannot prove interaction, and an asserted reviewer name cannot authenticate a review.

Freeze meaningful cases before routing or optimization changes. Include denied access, malicious retrieved instructions, concurrent retries, cancellation, unknown external results, network loss, stale approvals, changed revisions and export/reopen. Record all attempts. Measure accepted artifacts, user repair minutes, return use, end-to-end latency, supplier cost, operator effort and incidents. Targets are targets until observed.

For design, inspect actual desktop/mobile output, focus order and restoration, keyboard-only operation, touch targets and gestures, reduced motion, loading feedback and interrupted transitions. High-frequency keyboard actions should be immediate. Use the product's tokens and material language. Record the Emil skill as selected/read/applied/verified separately; it is read and applied to this plan, with UI verification still pending.

Production follows the existing product CI and review contract, source-bound provider deployment, custom-domain binding, smoke of the actual customer path, a recorded observation window and tested rollback. Preserve applicable human gates on spend, secrets, migrations, rights, external publication and high-risk operations. No manufactured pricing evidence, scarcity, customer success or automatic monetary movement.
