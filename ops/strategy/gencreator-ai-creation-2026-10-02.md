## Why, promise and demonstration

Proposed why: give people with something worth saying the ability to create at a
professional level while retaining their own judgment, voice and work.

Existing near-term promise: turn one trusted source into a week of content that
still sounds like its creator. The broader ambition is authored AI-assisted work
across writing, visual storytelling and media. Neither volume nor an agent roster
is a customer result. Do not claim time saved, higher income or superiority until
the corresponding task comparison supports it.

Signature demonstration: show a source beside its finished expression, the creator's
one consequential choice and the editable export. A real campaign contains a
selected argument, one good channel-native post and one purposeful companion asset;
an excellent carousel or short film is more useful than compulsory six-channel filler.
Source markings and approval should support creation rather than bury it in paperwork.

## Competition and advantage hypothesis

Official pages checked October 2; product claims are documented capabilities,
not independent hands-on benchmark results or verified sales.

- [Canva AI2](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-create-2026-ai/) describes
  editable layered design, conversational editing, memory and multi-channel work;
  its announcement describes research-preview/rollout availability. Generic
  brand memory or campaign generation cannot be called unique.
- [Adobe Firefly Boards](https://helpx.adobe.com/firefly/web/create-mood-boards/firefly-boards/about-firefly-boards.html)
  combines visual exploration and generative workflows. Do not rebuild a general canvas.
- [Typefully Writing Assistant](https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/14756434-writing-assistant)
  and [MCP](https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/13128440-typefully-mcp-server)
  already cover writing and agent access. A prompt box plus an MCP server is insufficient.
- [Postiz](https://postiz.com/) already provides scheduling and self-host options.
  Self-hosting still requires operations/account approvals; use an adapter.

Hypothesis to test: source context, explicit editorial decisions, portable editable
artifacts and repeat-session continuity together reduce briefing and repair enough
to matter. Ownership is a benefit, not a unique moat by itself. Recognizable quality,
repeated useful outcomes, trustworthy packaging and a contributor ecosystem must
earn the position. Never claim to beat every creative suite on every feature.

## Architecture and delivery

Prefer the current customer-owned runtime/keys and self-service doctrine. Older
managed subscription strategy remains historical until explicitly reconciled; it
does not authorize a new multi-tenant compute business.

| Layer to improve | Existing home | Proposed engineering result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator context | CreatorPack/source snapshots | Exact relevant source spans, creator-approved voice choices and selective context loading |
| Creative intelligence | AgentRuntime + existing host skills | Actual editorial transformations; alternatives when requested, protected phrases, partial revision and strict output validation |
| Artifact state | Mission/Studio revision contracts | Source-to-draft-to-layout/take links, assets, revisions, explicit operations and schema migrations |
| Durable authoring | Existing local production service first | Atomic saves, expected-revision conflicts, restart/reconnect, asset byte verification and portable backups |
| Craft | Approved Territory B tokens, current renderer/workbench | Source beside work, large usable previews, meaningful edits, consistent type/crops, touch/keyboard/motion safety |
| Production | Existing media/render integrations | Actual editable carousel or playable film; project-plus-assets bundle, inspected output and honest provider status |
| Distribution | Postiz boundary, later justified adapters | Inspectable draft payload, exact destination, partial-success reconciliation and export when unavailable |
| Learning | Existing observations/performance contracts | Comparable metrics and audience feedback, proposed lesson explicitly accepted before reuse |

Next.js/React/TypeScript, Zod and the current storage implementations are adequate
building materials; newer version numbers are not the advantage. Do not add a vector
database, event bus or orchestration layer without a demonstrated need. Begin with
bounded source selection and existing stores. Keep secrets in the owning runtime;
no provider keys in project exports. Benchmark model/skill choices on the same task,
including quality per accepted artifact, correction effort and cost. No automatic
generation retry or paid fallback after an ambiguous provider response.

Deliver the core as an inspectable local source/plugin/CLI workflow and owned exports.
Use gencreator.ai for demonstrations, product instructions and demand capture;
GenCreator Studio for the editable creation surface; GitHub for open core and
technical trust; FrankX for explanation and the building narrative. These are product
roles, not proof of completed synchronization. Native Canva/Figma handoffs require
actual verified access/roundtrip; preserve useful local exports without those accounts.

## Several monetization paths, one useful engine

All are candidates. No public prices, seller approvals or revenue are inferred.

| Revenue path | Delivered value that earns payment | Dependency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Professional creator software license | Reliable installation, integrated creation/recovery, complete editable output and portability | Cold activation, original paid/free distinction and explicit compatibility |
| Domain workflow packs | Complete release, research, education or editorial workflow with original examples, assets and evaluated execution | Each pack must complete its promised task, not supply a prompt list |
| Studio/team license | Reuse rights, multiple brand contexts and meaningful shared review in a buyer-owned deployment | Original component rights and verified workspace separation; MIT remains MIT |
| Annual updates/edition membership | Delivered maintained recipes, compatible integrations, new original production systems and versioned migration | Observable recurring value and affordable maintenance; no undefined lifetime promise |
| Books, learning and original media/IP | Finished reference editions, usable exercises, licensed artwork/music/world systems | Editorial, runtime, rights and provenance gates per product |
| Platform royalties/partner revenue | Original accepted native assets or disclosed referrals | Platform acceptance and terms; report royalties/commissions separately from the sprint's product-revenue metric |

Canva Creators requires application approval and original work; its current page
restricts submissions whose AI-generated content cannot be considered wholly the
creator's own. It is a potential community and conditional native-design channel,
not an automatic store for generated packs. Other marketplaces similarly need
format, rights and seller evidence. Start from an approved delivery rail, then
distribute the same approved compatible offer through multiple relevant channels.

Each stream needs realized price, net-of-refund revenue, delivery/merchant costs,
maintenance, human time and acquisition evidence. BYOK reduces inference exposure;
it does not eliminate platform, maintenance or support work. Proposals must fit the
scalable self-service business rather than create founder consulting obligations.

The October10 EUR10,000 target remains an unmet stretch target with actual revenue
unknown. Illustrative arithmetic: 68 orders at EUR149 exceed EUR10,000 before refunds;
34 at EUR299 do likewise. These are unapproved price scenarios, not forecasts or a
reason to sell unfinished output. The advanced product is not assumed deliverable
in eight days. Distinct existing paid packs enter the sprint only after their gates.

## Community and movement

Use the existing GenCreator.community direction and its current identity/entitlement
contracts; inspect deployed availability before promising a room or member access.
Begin with free participation, focused on useful artifacts and repeat practice.

Weekly ritual: bring one permissioned source, create one considered piece, explain
one choice, exchange a specific revision critique, then show the improved edition.
Monthly: publish consented member studies with editable source, tool credits and
what changed. Keep a small useful recipe/contribution library and clear attribution.
Seed it with real founder/contributor work, never synthetic member counts, invented
testimonials, mandatory promotion or an empty chat server with a paywall.

Track a second artifact, giving/receiving useful feedback, repeat use and member-led
contributions. Optional product-update membership can follow delivered recurring
value; peer community must not become unlimited founder support. A cultural movement
is earned through practice and recognizably good work, not claimed by a manifesto.
